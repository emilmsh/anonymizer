# Interview with the finance director of Lyngheim municipality

*Held on Teams on 3 April 2026. Notes: Ola Nordmann.*

## About the municipality

Lyngheim has about 40,000 inhabitants. We received the grant in 2023, the same year we merged the finance departments of North Lyngheim and South Lyngheim.

## Experience with the scheme

The application was demanding. We used external consultants from Fjordråd AS, and it cost us NOK 350,000.

> "Without the grant we would not have got the new finance system in place," says the finance director.

The scheme is run by the directorate, and contact with the case officer there has been good.
