Interview – evaluation of the grant scheme
Date: 12 March 2026
Interviewer: Ola Nordmann
Respondent: Kari Nordmann, Fjordvik municipality

I: Could you tell me a little about your job?
R: I am the only lawyer in child welfare in Fjordvik municipality. There are four of us in the service, and I have been here since 1998.
I: What is it like to be the only lawyer in Fjordvik?
R: Busy. Fjordvik is small, so everybody knows everybody. After the inspection case last year, which Fjordvik-Posten wrote a lot about, the pressure grew.
I: What has the grant meant for you?
R: We got the grant in 2024 and used it on a new case management system, Modulus Barn. It has saved us a lot of double entry.
I: How is the cooperation with the management?
R: My manager Per Hansen has been supportive, also when I was on sick leave with burnout for three months last year.
I: Is there anything you would like to add?
R: No. Feel free to send the notes to kari.nordmann@fjordvik.kommune.no.
